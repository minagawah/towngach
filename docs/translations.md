# Translations in Parenthesis

Throughout the document, many words are associated
with translations enclosed in parenthesis.
Like, **Feng-Shui (風水 / 风水 / phong thủy)**,
for insntance, you can immediately tell
**"Feng-Shui"** being the alphabetical representation
of Chinese pronunciation.

For translations, we have the following:
- **ja** (Japanese)
- **zh_tw** (Traditional Chinese)
- **zh_ch** (Simplified Chinese)
- **vi** (Vietnamese)

However, in many occasions, **"zh_tw"** and
**"ja"** shares the same characters (**"風水"**, etc.)
and they are often combined into 1 (that is why
there are only 3 above).
